# Client delivery and marketing quality checklist

## Before accepting a project
- [ ] Obtain written business authorization, goal, target market and intended users
- [ ] List client-provided materials, copyrights, brand assets, and access ownership
- [ ] Confirm a precise deliverable, deadline, one revision round, accepted formats and exclusions
- [ ] Quote a fixed setup fee and any recurring fees; pass-through ad spend is separate
- [ ] Agree payment terms and review/approval process
- [ ] Choose a private project repository; never place credentials in commit history

## Websites
- [ ] Clear primary call-to-action and contact details
- [ ] Responsive/mobile testing, keyboard navigation, contrast, alt text
- [ ] Functional contact/booking form with appropriate spam protection
- [ ] Privacy and cookie handling appropriate to deployed analytics/forms
- [ ] Performance test, working links, no placeholder claims, contact route tested
- [ ] Domain/hosting access controlled by client or contract; backup + portability

## Social and videos
- [ ] Verify rights to footage, music, brand/people likenesses, model outputs and stock assets
- [ ] Check correct spelling, readable captions, platform aspect ratios, realistic claims
- [ ] Obtain specific final customer approval before scheduling or publishing
- [ ] Test playback on phone; deliver original and export; keep a source archive
- [ ] Report output and meaningful business metrics; never claim guaranteed results

## Monthly service operations
- [ ] Content request by day 1, draft batch by day 5, feedback cutoff by day 8
- [ ] Plan queue at least seven days in advance; emergency content separately scoped
- [ ] End-of-month report includes published posts, total production time, leads if trackable, next action
- [ ] Track churn/renewals and revise package if effective hourly profit is inadequate

## AI agent safety gate (future)
- [ ] Use nonproduction test data first, isolated workspace and limited permissions
- [ ] Require human approval for emails, posting, deleting, purchases, deploys and phone campaigns
- [ ] Log actions; establish emergency stop and human call transfer
- [ ] Disclose AI interactions where appropriate; examine consent, recording and telemarketing rules before voice deployment
