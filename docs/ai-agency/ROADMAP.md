# Practical execution roadmap
**Priority rule:** No new subscriptions or hardware until one paid pilot demonstrates demand.

## Recovery / admin window (no deadline, protect rest)
- Safely export and back up business files; do not reinstall Windows impulsively.
- Verify Bolt subscriptions and billing; cancel duplicate/unneeded seats only after exporting work.
- Review this GitHub planning branch. No project is deployed by this branch.

## First available focused session (60-90 minutes)
1. Check GPU and free disk space.
2. Run/install Ollama and test a small local language model.
3. Install one video editor (Kdenlive).
4. Finish one truthful demo-case-study page; mark LCM clearly as portfolio concept.
5. Capture actual production time, screenshots and problems.

## Next two sessions
- Compare same website task in Bolt.new and local bolt.diy/Dyad.
- Export code and host a client-preview deployment under owner-controlled account.
- Produce a 15-30 second service ad from assets with usage rights; no AI video generator necessary.

## First two weeks of outreach
- Assemble offer, proposal, sample, contact sheet and simple leads spreadsheet.
- Pitch 20 relevant local businesses one at a time, including qualified warm introductions.
- Follow up once with permission, record real responses. Never automate spam.
- Close one paid pilot and deliver it excellently before adding advanced systems.

## Future milestones
**Stage 2:** Customer content calendar, metrics and standardized monthly packages.
**Stage 3:** Lead intake and follow-up workflows after processes are measured.
**Stage 4:** Inbound AI receptionist demo with consent/privacy review, human fallback and uptime plan.
**Stage 5:** Explore hosted AI apps only when a validated user need justifies infrastructure/support cost.

## What NOT to do first
- No public 24/7 AI voice service from a laptop.
- No automated outbound sales robocalls or unapproved AI voice recordings.
- No model training from scratch.
- No 4 TB SSD until model benchmarks or backups demonstrate need.
- No privileged computer agent on personal files, browsers, finance accounts or production repos without sandboxing and explicit approvals.
