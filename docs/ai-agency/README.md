# AI-Assisted Small-Business Marketing Studio
**Status:** Planning / portfolio demonstration. **Goal:** Validate one paying client before taking on subscriptions or expensive hardware.

## What we sell (phase 1)
1. Mobile-first service-business websites and landing pages with clear calls to action, quote forms, accessibility checks, analytics and domain handoff.
2. Client-approved short-form video editing and branded images, generally 15-60 seconds, preferably based on genuine client footage.
3. Limited, measurable monthly content packages; no guaranteed leads or unverified performance claims.

**Not selling yet:** Autonomous 24/7 voice receptionists, fully automatic outbound calls, heavy model training, or a public hosted LLM SaaS. These require additional testing, infrastructure and privacy/legal review.

## Example offers for validation (not market benchmarks)
| Offer | Trial price | Scope |
|---|---:|---|
| One-page landing site | $350-650 | Copy + responsive build + form + deployment handoff, fixed revisions |
| Short video ad | $75-150 | 15-60 s from supplied footage/photos + one revision |
| Starter content | $249/month | 8 posts + 1 edited video + scheduling draft/report |
| Growth content | $399/month | 12 posts + 2 edited videos + simple report |

Paid ad spend, domains, hosting, API use, complex automation, and extra revisions are **excluded** and quoted separately. Do not spend client ad budgets from personal accounts.

## Launch sequence
1. Protect the computer: back up data, inspect partitions, verify NVIDIA GPU and storage. No Windows reinstall, resizing or destructive disk commands until verified backups.
2. Make one credible portfolio case study (Lancaster Consolidated Maintenance **demo**, not a claim of partnership, clients or results).
3. Build two reusable templates and a 30-second video assembled from licensed/client-authorized footage.
4. Create intake form, written scope, change/revision policy, payment terms and client approval checklist.
5. Reach out personally to 20 local service businesses and track contact, needs, response, quote and outcome.
6. Sell one paid pilot; log actual hours and costs. Adjust offer before adding more software.

## Rules
- Never fabricate reviews, before/after results, client outcomes, ROI or projects.
- Obtain rights to customer images, logos, faces, music and voice; verify each AI model/tool's commercial license.
- Never commit private customer data, passwords, secrets or tokens to GitHub.
- Keep work in separate client repositories with limited access, and don't depend on one website-builder's hosting.
- Customer reviews/approvals are required before publishing ads or scheduling posts.
- Measure qualified inquiries, booked jobs, and client retention, not only likes and views.
- For agents: use least-privilege access, sandbox/containers, manual review of file changes and no autonomous payments, deletions, mass messaging or deployments.
